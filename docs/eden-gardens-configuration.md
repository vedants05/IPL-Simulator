# Eden Gardens evidence and rendering decisions

Verified 2026-09-07. The starting model represents the existing ground, not announced future redevelopment.

## Confirmed

- CAB, 31 March 2023: eight corporate boxes in B Block, seven in L Block; Club House boxes also upgraded. Tempered glass fronts are described for the press box. https://www.cricketassociationofbengal.com/newspage/news2401.php
- CAB's tender catalogue lists tensile-membrane roof work on F/G/H and the Club House. A tender is not proof of completion. https://www.cricketassociationofbengal.com/tender.php
- INI describes metal-clad roof structures, suites, press booths and concessions between ground and upper concourses. https://inidesignstudio.com/portfolio/eden-garden-cricket-stadium/

## Corrections implemented

B's two modules display four boxes each; L displays seven. These facilities are independent of the seating template, preserving current seating capacity and upgrade economics. Existing unmodified historical B/L modules receive the correction without rewriting stand names, capacity, projects or roof settings. Replacement stands use the chosen template's facilities.

All fourteen upgrade templates have explicit visual definitions: tier count, seat spacing, suite count and facade style. Roof selections distinguish open sky, short canopy, full canopy, cantilever beams and raised segmented landmark panels. Condition changes surface appearance; elite quality changes finishes and seat spacing. Construction displays exposed terraces and scaffolding.

## Unverified / artistic approximations

Exact roof coverage, tier counts, block positions, construction years and per-block seat counts in the original prototype have not been established by these sources. No blanket change from partial to full roofs has been made. The High Court Pavilion label and L's heritage designation remain legacy approximations. Default fan opinions and condition values are game parameters. Pavilion suite quantities other than B/L and all upgrade geometry are illustrative, not surveyed.

Do not describe this as an exact reconstruction or turn tenders/future proposals into completed structures. Existing circular module footprints are retained pending a trustworthy measured seating/roof plan.
