## Rating rationale

- **Monty Desai:** Nepal's ODI revival and World Cup qualification support strong development, tactical and man-management ratings; Canada tenure ended in August 2026.
- **Shaun Tait:** Specialist pace coaching and Bangladesh's improved pace unit support a high pace rating; head-coach skills remain more modest.
- **Luke Ronchi:** Long New Zealand batting-coach tenure and wicketkeeping background justify dual specialism; new BBL head role raises leadership potential.
- **Kevin Pietersen:** Elite batting experience and current England mentoring remit support batting, reputation and motivation; short formal coaching tenure limits other skills.
- **Matthew Mott:** Multiple senior head-coach roles and international trophies support strong tactics and management, with a meaningful but narrower batting specialism.
- **Darren Lehmann:** Long national, franchise and county head-coaching record supports leadership and batting expertise; he is under a current county contract.
- **Troy Cooley:** ECB national pace lead and long academy record justify elite pace, technical and youth-development scores.
- **Jonty Rhodes:** Long-running IPL fielding appointments and specialist reputation support elite fielding; no unsupported 2026 IPL contract is assumed.
- **Ravi Shastri:** Successful India head-coach tenure supports top-tier leadership and motivation; availability is an in-game market assumption, not a claimed current appointment.
- **Chris Silverwood:** England and Sri Lanka head-coaching jobs plus earlier England pace role support both leadership and pace ratings.
- **Marcus Trescothick:** ECB elite batting role, interim white-ball leadership and continuing England staff presence support batting and management ratings.
- **Sarah Taylor:** Elite wicketkeeping career and demonstrated men's specialist coaching justify elite keeping and fielding, with growing wider coaching ability.
